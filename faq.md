# Synthetic Healthcare Support FAQ

This file contains fictional clinic policies for demonstration purposes only. It is not a real healthcare organization's policy.

## Cancellation policy

Patients should cancel or reschedule appointments as early as possible. For this demo, cancellations made at least 24 hours before the appointment do not incur a cancellation fee.

## Clinic hours

The fictional clinic is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## Insurance questions

The clinic can help patients understand the insurance information available to the clinic, but coverage and patient responsibility are determined by the patient's insurance plan.

## Medical advice

The support assistant does not diagnose conditions, recommend medications, interpret symptoms, or replace a healthcare professional.

## Prescription refills

Prescription refill requests should be directed to the prescribing provider or the clinic's designated refill process. The assistant does not approve or prescribe medications.

## Records requests

Patients can request copies of their records through the clinic's records process. The assistant can explain the process but cannot release protected health information.

## Emergency situations

The assistant is not an emergency service. For a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or seek appropriate emergency care.
